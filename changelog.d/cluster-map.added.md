- **A map of the cluster.** Map, under Workloads (or G then Y), draws everything in the namespaces
  picked as one picture: what routes traffic, the services, the workloads, their pods and what they
  use or mount, with lines between them, in a band per namespace, node or app. Each card says how
  it is in words, each band counts what's failing or warns, and Find dims what doesn't match. On a
  large cluster or a narrow window, pods are counted on their workloads and what's healthy folds
  away; it can also be shown as a list.
